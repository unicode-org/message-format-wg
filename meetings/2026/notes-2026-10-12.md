## [Issue \#1117](https://github.com/unicode-org/message-format-wg/issues/1117), Align datetime formatting options with other standards

EAO: one of the things that is possibly happening is formattable time element in HTML. Not related to DOM Localization, but a separate thing. The way it would work is `<time>` element could have attributes that declare ISO date is this or that. 

… given that we’re the most recent people to look into this. [Luca Casonato](mailto:hello@lcas.dev) is pushing this. All the things we have in :datetime are included (or should be) in the `<time>` element. Links what is in MF2 and HTML and JS. For date/time formatting is something that you can implement with current JS options. But some are not spelled the same. Now we end up with situation where having MF options become more directly part of web stack of things. Interest in having set of stuff that works in HTML also work in JS. Which means MF2 should work in JS. But the couple of places where the matches are not perfect. 

MIH: maybe drag shane more into this. What we have should be to get EMCAScript people to use same things. 

EAO: shane is aware. Separate proposal for ecma 402\. Little bit of alignment that we should discuss. We have a field `dateLength`; having `dateStyle` instead would be more in alignment, also internally consistent with `timezoneStyle`

APP: have action item to comment on the html issue.

EAO: would we consider `dateLength` etc. to `dateStyle` etc. 

MIH: would do what cldr does.
